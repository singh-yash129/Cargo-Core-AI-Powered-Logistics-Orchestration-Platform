DESIGN OF COMPONENTS
=====================
Software: Cargo-Core | Team: QuadCore-Devs (Team-003)


• Customer Component (Booking & Tracking APIs)

   o Submit a structured booking request with goods type, quantity, pickup and delivery location
   o Receive a digital booking confirmation with order ID, cost breakdown and service details
   o Get system-suggested vehicle size and labourer count based on cargo details
   o Track real-time delivery status of active orders without calling the dispatcher
   o Record full or partial payments and view remaining balance for each order
   o View all past orders, quotes, and damage reports from the customer dashboard
   o Submit damage reports with photo evidence and receive QR-coded acknowledgement
   o Request price quotes before confirming a booking


• Owner / Logistic Manager Component (Finance, Monitoring & Operations APIs)

   o View auto-generated digital invoices for every completed order
   o Track full, partial, and pending payments across all customers to monitor outstanding dues
   o Monitor real-time vehicle locations across the fleet without calling drivers
   o View daily, weekly, and monthly logistics reports and revenue dashboards
   o Track labour attendance and working hours for accurate payroll calculation
   o Generate weekly payroll summaries per labourer with transparent wage breakdowns
   o Compare vehicle mileage against submitted fuel expenses to detect anomalies
   o Prioritize and manage orders during peak season demand
   o Monitor all communication between customers, drivers, and dispatcher in one place
   o Manage warehouse registrations, fleet, zones, and user accounts


• Dispatcher Component (Resource Allocation & Scheduling APIs)

   o Receive AI-powered recommendations for vehicle type and labourer count per order
   o Generate and share daily work schedules for labourers before each shift
   o View estimated loading and unloading time predictions for each order
   o Digitally confirm equipment issued and returned for each job to prevent disputes
   o Manage pending dispatch queue and assign orders to available drivers
   o Balance workload across drivers using load balancing and order clustering
   o Track real-time driver status (active, idle, on-route, completed)
   o Handle crisis situations such as vehicle breakdowns or driver unavailability
   o Optimize delivery routes across multiple orders for minimum fuel usage
   o Manage communication and coordination between warehouse and field teams


• Warehouse Manager Component (Inventory & Operations APIs)

   o Map and organize storage layout with sections, aisles, shelves, and bin locations
   o Receive automatic low-stock alerts for packing materials and inventory items
   o Record issued and returned equipment (crates, belts, blankets) per order
   o Manage inbound orders and assign them to warehouse zones for picking
   o Execute picking process — generate pick lists, assign pickers, track progress per SKU
   o Manage packing stations and track items packed per station per day
   o Assign loading docks to trucks and track dock availability in real time
   o Perform quality checks before dispatch (goods count, packaging, labelling, weight)
   o Grade and process returned goods with condition assessment and refund calculation
   o Monitor warehouse floor plan with zone-wise capacity and throughput metrics
   o Track labourer availability and assign them to active orders


• Driver Component (Field Execution & Delivery APIs)

   o Receive complete job details including cargo type, pickup address, delivery address and OTP
   o Get optimized navigation routes to minimize fuel usage and travel time
   o Record digital start and end work times for transparent payment calculation
   o Update delivery status at each stage (picked up, in-transit, delivered, failed)
   o Sync delivery status updates even in low network or offline conditions
   o Confirm equipment receipt and return at pickup and delivery sites
   o View assigned order history and delivery performance stats


• Inventory Management Component (Backend APIs)

   o Create and manage inventory items per warehouse with SKU, category, unit, and location
   o Track real-time stock levels and trigger safety stock alerts automatically
   o Record every inventory movement (inbound, outbound, transfer) with order references
   o Generate pick lists for orders based on available stock
   o Create and manage restock requests with approval workflow
   o Enforce warehouse-level inventory isolation for multi-warehouse operations


• Labour & Workforce Component (Backend APIs)

   o Register labourers and assign them to specific warehouses with skill tags
   o Record daily attendance through digital check-in and check-out events
   o Assign labourers to active orders based on skill and availability
   o Track equipment issued to each labourer per job for accountability
   o Generate weekly payroll data based on recorded attendance and work hours
   o Monitor active vs inactive labourer availability in real time


• Authentication & Access Control Component (Auth APIs)

   o Register new users with role selection (Individual, Vendor, Warehouse Manager, Dispatcher)
   o Login with email and password with JWT access and refresh token issuance
   o Google OAuth 2.0 login for quick access
   o Email OTP verification before completing signup
   o Role-based dashboard redirection after successful login
   o Forgot password and secure reset password via email token
   o Token refresh and automatic session management
   o Logout with token blacklisting via Redis


• AI Intelligence Component (AI & Analytics APIs)

   o Accept natural language questions from logistics managers about operations
   o Generate and execute SQL queries from plain English using Gemini AI
   o Return formatted, human-readable answers from live database data
   o Maintain session-based conversation history for context-aware responses
   o Classify query intent (database query, general question, greeting)
   o Escalate unresolved or sensitive queries to a human agent automatically
   o Track escalation status from open to resolved


• Communication & Coordination Component (Real-time APIs)

   o Provide a centralized chat system between dispatchers, drivers, and warehouse teams
   o Send and receive messages within warehouse-specific communication threads
   o Create and manage logistics alerts with severity levels and recommendations
   o Track and resolve operational escalations raised by any team member
   o Send notifications for order status changes, assignment updates, and alerts
   o Log all communication for audit and dispute resolution purposes
